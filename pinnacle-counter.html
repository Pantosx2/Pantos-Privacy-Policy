This privacy policy applies specifically to Pinnacle-Πινάκλ Counter, provided by Pantos. The app helps you keep score for a Pinnacle game and read the game rules. You do not need an account.

Player names, scores and settings

The player or team names you enter, target score, round scores, round history, current game state and language preference are processed and saved locally on your device so you can keep score and continue a game. You can use nicknames instead of real names. The game rules are bundled with the app and read locally.

Collection, use and sharing

Pinnacle-Πινάκλ Counter does not upload your player names, scores or settings to the developer or third parties. It does not collect personal information on developer-operated servers, sell user data, or include advertising, analytics or developer-operated crash reporting services. It does not request camera, microphone, contacts or location access. The exception for information you voluntarily send by email is explained below.

Retention, deletion and security

Saved game data and preferences remain in local app storage until they are overwritten or the app's storage is cleared. To remove all locally saved player names, scores, game history and settings, use Android Settings → Apps → Pinnacle-Πινάκλ Counter → Storage → Clear storage/data. Uninstalling the app removes its local app data. Device backups may retain or restore app data depending on your operating system and backup settings; manage those copies through your backup provider's settings.

Local files are kept in the app's storage area and protected by the device's operating system access controls. The app does not provide additional encryption for saved names and scores. Protect your device and any backups you enable. We do not have remote access to your locally saved game data.

Contacting the developer

If you email pantosxpantos@gmail.com, Pantos receives your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. This information is used to answer your request, provide support or address a privacy concern. It is processed through our email provider, Google, and is not used for advertising. Please avoid sending unnecessary personal information.

Support correspondence is kept only as long as needed to handle the request and meet applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion by email; information may be retained where the law requires it.

External websites and this policy page

Opening an external link sends a request from your browser to that website. This policy is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs visitors' IP addresses for security. This website hosting is separate from data processed locally by the app. We have not added advertising, analytics scripts, cookies or forms to these policy pages. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for GitHub's handling of website visits. Other external services are governed by their own privacy policies.

Children's privacy

The app's local processing described above applies to users of all ages. The app does not ask children to provide personal information to the developer. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us, contact us so we can address it and delete it where appropriate.

Your privacy choices and rights

You control the app's local data through your device settings. We cannot access or remotely delete data held only on your device. For personal information you send to us by email, you may request access, correction or deletion, or exercise other rights available under applicable law, by contacting us. Where applicable, you may object to processing or complain to your local data protection authority. We handle support correspondence to respond to your inquiry, based on our legitimate interest in providing support, and retain information where required by law.

Changes to this policy

Updates will be published on this page with a revised date. Material changes to app data handling will also be reflected in the app and store disclosures as appropriate.
