Cube Solver — Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 24 November 2026

App: Cube Solver

Android package: com.pantosxpantos.cube_solver

Developer on Google Play: Pantos

Developer details: Pantos Pantos, Greece

Privacy contact: pantosxpantos@gmail.com

This privacy policy applies specifically to Cube Solver, provided by Pantos. Cube Solver helps you enter or scan cube colors and follow a solution. It works without an account, and cube scanning and solving take place on your device.

Camera access and photos

If you choose to scan a cube, the app requests camera permission and uses the camera preview and captured images to identify sticker colors. Images and derived color samples are processed on your device. The app does not upload them or share them with the developer or third parties, and it does not record audio. You can deny or revoke camera permission in your device settings and enter colors manually instead.

Captured photos may remain in the app's local temporary cache until the app cache is cleared or the operating system removes them. The app does not intentionally save scans to your photo gallery.

Saved cube state and local storage

The app saves the cube's sticker colors and current state in its local app storage so you can return to your cube. Scanned color samples may also be held in memory while you enter and review the cube. Solving calculations and move previews run locally. This information is not sent to a server.

Collection, use and sharing

Cube Solver does not collect personal information on developer-operated servers, sell user data, or include advertising, analytics or developer-operated crash reporting services. It does not request your name, email address, contacts, location or an account to scan or solve a cube. Camera images and cube state are used only to provide the scanning, editing and solving features described here. The exception for information you voluntarily send by email is explained below.

Retention, deletion and security

Saved cube state remains in local app storage until it is overwritten or the app's storage is cleared. You can remove saved cube data and cached scans through Android Settings → Apps → Cube Solver → Storage → Clear storage/data; clearing the cache removes cached files. Uninstalling the app removes its local app data. Device backups may retain or restore app data depending on your operating system and backup settings; manage those copies through your backup provider's settings.

Local files are kept in the app's storage area and protected by the device's operating system access controls. The app does not provide additional encryption for saved cube state. Protect your device and any backups you enable. We do not have remote access to your locally saved cube or scans.

Contacting the developer

If you email pantosxpantos@gmail.com, Pantos receives your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. This information is used to answer your request, provide support or address a privacy concern. It is processed through our email provider, Google, and is not used for advertising. Please avoid sending unnecessary personal information.

Support correspondence is kept only as long as needed to handle the request and meet applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion by email; information may be retained where the law requires it.

External websites and this policy page

Opening an external link sends a request from your browser to that website. This policy is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs visitors' IP addresses for security. This website hosting is separate from data processed locally by the app. We have not added advertising, analytics scripts, cookies or forms to these policy pages. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for GitHub's handling of website visits. Other external services are governed by their own privacy policies.

Children's privacy

The app's local processing described above applies to users of all ages. The app does not ask children to provide personal information to the developer. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us, contact us so we can address it and delete it where appropriate.

Your privacy choices and rights

You control the app's local data through your device settings. We cannot access or remotely delete data held only on your device. For personal information you send to us by email, you may request access, correction or deletion, or exercise other rights available under applicable law, by contacting us. Where applicable, you may object to processing or complain to your local data protection authority. We handle support correspondence to respond to your inquiry, based on our legitimate interest in providing support, and retain information where required by law.

Changes to this policy

Updates will be published on this page with a revised date. Material changes to app data handling will also be reflected in the app and store disclosures as appropriate.
